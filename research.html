Research Overview

My research focuses on the application of computational chemistry, molecular modelling and cheminformatics to investigate chemical and biological systems. I am interested in using computational methods to understand molecular interactions, reaction mechanisms and processes relevant to drug discovery.

Research Areas

Computational Chemistry and DFT

Quantum chemical calculations, molecular properties, electronic structure and theoretical investigations of reaction mechanisms.

Computer-Aided Drug Discovery

Molecular docking, structure-based virtual screening, ligand evaluation and computational drug design.

Molecular Dynamics Simulation

Investigation of molecular stability, conformational changes and protein–ligand interactions through molecular dynamics simulations.

Cheminformatics

Chemical information analysis, molecular descriptors, fingerprints, chemical similarity and computational approaches to chemical data.

Network Pharmacology

Computational investigation of compound–target relationships, biological pathways and molecular networks relevant to drug discovery.

Molecular Modelling

Application of computational tools to explore molecular structures, interactions and properties.
