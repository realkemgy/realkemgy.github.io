Welcome to My Teaching Page

This section provides learning materials and academic resources in physical chemistry, molecular spectroscopy, quantum chemistry, computational chemistry and cheminformatics.

My goal is to support students in developing a strong understanding of chemical principles and their practical applications.

Courses

Understanding molecular structure and properties using spectroscopic techniques.

Quantum Chemistry

Quantum mechanical foundations of atoms, molecules and chemical bonding.

Computational Chemistry

Computational methods for studying molecular structures, energies and reactions.

Chemical representations, molecular descriptors, fingerprints and similarity analysis.

Cheminformatics II

Applications of chemical data analysis, virtual screening and drug discovery.
