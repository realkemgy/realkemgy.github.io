Course Overview

This course introduces students to the fundamental principles of chemical kinetics, with emphasis on the rates of chemical reactions, the factors that influence reaction rates and the mechanisms through which chemical reactions occur.

It examines experimental and theoretical approaches to understanding reaction rates, rate laws, reaction order, integrated rate equations, temperature dependence of reaction rates and reaction mechanisms.

Students will develop the ability to analyse kinetic data, determine rate equations and understand the relationship between reaction mechanisms and observed reaction behaviour.

Course Aim

To develop a sound understanding of the fundamental principles governing the rates and mechanisms of chemical reactions and the application of kinetic theories to chemical processes.

Learning Objectives

Students completing this course should be able to:

  1. Explain the fundamental concepts and principles of chemical kinetics.
  2. Define reaction rate and identify factors affecting reaction rates.
  3. Determine rate laws and reaction orders from experimental data.
  4. Apply integrated rate equations to analyse reaction kinetics.
  5. Explain the effects of temperature on reaction rates.
  6. Apply the Arrhenius equation to determine activation energy.
  7. Explain the principles of collision theory and transition state theory.
  8. Describe reaction mechanisms and identify rate-determining steps.
  9. Analyse experimental kinetic data using appropriate mathematical models.

Course Content

  1. Introduction to Chemical Kinetics
  2. Reaction Rates and Rate Expressions
  3. Rate Laws and Reaction Order
  4. Integrated Rate Equations
  5. Half-Life of Chemical Reactions
  6. Temperature Dependence of Reaction Rates
  7. The Arrhenius Equation and Activation Energy
  8. Collision Theory of Reaction Rates
  9. Transition State Theory
  10. Reaction Mechanisms and Elementary Reactions
  11. Catalysis and Catalytic Reactions
