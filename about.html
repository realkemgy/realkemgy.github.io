Academic Profile

I am an Associate Professor with academic and research interests in computational chemistry, cheminformatics and computer-aided drug discovery. My work focuses on applying computational and molecular modelling approaches to understand chemical and biological systems.

My academic activities combine university teaching, research supervision and scientific investigation. I am particularly interested in making computational methods accessible to students and researchers through structured learning resources and practical applications.

Areas of Specialisation

  • Computational Chemistry
  • Cheminformatics
  • Computer-Aided Drug Discovery
  • Molecular Modelling and Simulation
  • Density Functional Theory (DFT)

Teaching Interests

My teaching interests include Physical Chemistry, Molecular Spectroscopy, Quantum Chemistry, Computational Chemistry and Cheminformatics.

Research Interests

My research interests include computational studies of reaction mechanisms, molecular docking, molecular dynamics simulations, structure-based drug discovery and chemical data analysis.

Academic Mission

To advance chemical sciences through effective teaching, computational research, scientific collaboration and the development of practical learning resources.
